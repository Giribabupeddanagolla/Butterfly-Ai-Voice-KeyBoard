The Android IME Pipeline

How Butterfly AI transforms your voice into formatted text inside any app:

1
User taps text field Focus active input field in any Android application.
2
Butterfly AI keyboard opens System launches Butterfly AI as your default input method engine.
3
User speaks Natural speech stream captured in your preferred vernacular or English.
4
Speech becomes text Whisper & fast speech-to-text neural models transcribe words instantly.
5
Translation / AI processing Optional live translation, grammar polishing, or AI answering.
6
Result inserted into active text field Final polished text commits directly to your app's cursor location.

WhatsApp Gmail Chrome Instagram Telegram Notes
